# regret-liste

Mode : génération d’images intégrée. Nouvelle création.

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one finished square French Facebook ad in a radically different native format: overhead candid photograph of ONE large cream handwritten task-list sheet on an ordinary wooden family kitchen table. The paper fills90% frame, nearly straight perspective, big readable handwriting. No person or crying face, no navy lower panel, no glossy banner template. Exact headline at top of paper, dark ink thick neat lettering: "EST-CE VRAIMENT CE QUE VOUS VOULEZ LEUR LAISSER ?". Below smaller heading "SUCCESSION", then three large EMPTY hand-drawn checkbox lines: "Comprendre les papiers", "Trouver l’argent", "Se mettre d’accord". A spare pair of reading glasses and a pen at paper edge suggest absent parent, no other props or photo needed. Bottom of paper one short printed sentence: "Vos enfants auront déjà votre absence à affronter.". Beneath that a compact orange #E8730C strip with navy text "DÉCOUVRIR LES 7 ERREURS À ÉVITER" and little arrow, clearly ad CTA integrated neatly. Tactile paper, soft natural window shadows, restrained dark-red underline under "LEUR LAISSER", emotionally immediate. Large clear mobile legibility for French parents60-80. No solutions disclosed, no invented stats or deadlines. No brand name, logo, signatures, real testimony, 'scène illustrative' label, em dash or decorative wording. Only specified words. Sparse hierarchy, no repeated text.
