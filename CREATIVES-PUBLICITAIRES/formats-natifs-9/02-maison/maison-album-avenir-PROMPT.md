# maison-album-avenir

Mode : génération d’images intégrée. Nouvelle création.

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one finished square French Facebook ad in tactile family-photo-album native format, not standard photo plus navy lower panel. Entire frame cream textured album spread viewed almost directly overhead, generous simple margins. Headline printed large dark charcoal on top cream margin exactly "LEUR MAISON D’ENFANCE. DEVRONT-ILS LUI DIRE ADIEU ?". Center has two large side-by-side printed photographs mounted with old photo corners: left genuinely 1980s color snapshot of two children playing in front of ordinary French family house with pale shutters and red tile roof, parents in background; right EXACT same house façade and architectural details decades later, subdued daylight, empty garden with visible natural sized À VENDRE sign on gate. Children absent in right picture. No impossible buildings or mismatched architecture. Left photo caption "LEURS SOUVENIRS", right caption "LEUR AVENIR ?". Contrast warm old memory vs uncertain sale, not guaranteed event. Below photos short large sentence in dark ink "Et si les droits de succession les obligeaient à vendre ?". Small strong brick-red CTA rectangle on cream footer exactly "VOIR LES 7 ERREURS À ÉVITER", white readable text. No navy panel, no generic crying stock portrait, no giant hand or excess props. Photo realism on prints, correct perspective and believable materials. Text minimal and highly readable for older adults. No brand/logo, faux media, guarantees, numbers, deadlines, decorative sayings, scene illustrative label or em dash. Only specified text. 6% safe margins. Distinctive concise scrapbook style with compelling emotional loss contrast.
