# Maison : moyens de la garder

Mode : génération d’images intégrée, nouvelle création.

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one finished square French inheritance Facebook advertisement. Upper 60% a believable documentary photograph of a woman around48 sitting at an old wooden dining table inside her parents' modest French home. She looks worried at a normal-sized document titled 'Droits de succession', resting flat on the table beside a small ordinary calculator angled toward her. Keys to the house clearly visible in foreground beside a small aged photograph of her as a child with her two parents in front of this same house. Logical desk arrangement, nothing floating, anatomically natural hands (one lightly resting by calculator, other on paper). No exaggerated tears. Plain brown shoulder-length hair in coherent natural soft locks, no wiry AI flyaways, natural unretouched skin and soft window light. Quiet domestic surroundings no luxury or stylization. Emotional key idea: owning keys without enough cash to keep childhood home. No À VENDRE sign, no elderly parent physically in main scene, no envelope held at gate. No Heritage Intact name or logos. No invented monetary amounts, countdowns, official stamps, or testimonials. Absolutely NO decorative text on walls, objects, frames, fridge, mugs. No 'scène illustrative' label.
